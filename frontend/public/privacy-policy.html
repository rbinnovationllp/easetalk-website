Privacy Policy – EaseTalk Alert

Rashi Bhartiya Innovation LLP (“we”, “our”, “us”) built EaseTalk Alert to assist users, especially individuals with hearing impairments, by detecting important sounds and alerts.

1. Information We Collect

  • Microphone Access: Used only for detecting sounds like name calls, doorbell, baby cry, and alerts. Audio is not stored permanently.
  • User Saved Name: Stored locally on your device for name detection.
  • Custom Sounds: If uploaded, stored locally on device only.

2. How We Use Information

We use this data only to provide alert functionality such as vibration, popup notifications, and sound alerts.

3. Data Storage

  • No audio recordings are stored or shared.
  • All user data remains on the device.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone – for sound detection
  • Storage (optional) – for custom sounds
